About Our Moving Company

Making Moving in Uganda More Organized

OLLY MOVERS is a Uganda-based moving and relocation company focused on helping individuals, families, students, offices and businesses move their belongings from one place to another with care and proper planning.

A move is more than transporting furniture from one address to another. It involves planning the pickup, understanding the destination, considering access and stairs, organizing belongings, loading them carefully, transporting them and making sure everything reaches the intended destination in an organized way.

That is the approach behind OLLY MOVERS. We encourage customers to provide the important details of their move early so that the moving arrangement can be planned around the actual requirements of the job.

Our Story

We Understand the Customer

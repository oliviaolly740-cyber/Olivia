Need To Know

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I request a quotation?

Complete the move-request form above or contact us directly on WhatsApp. Share your pickup location, destination, moving date, items and any special requirements.

Do you move outside Kampala?

Yes. OLLY MOVERS is Uganda-based and can discuss planned moves to destinations outside Kampala, depending on the route, load and transport requirements.

Can you help with packing?

Yes. Packing and unpacking can be arranged depending on the needs of your move. Tell us what needs to be packed when requesting your quote.

What information should I provide for a quote?

Provide your name and contact, pickup and destination areas, preferred date, type of move, the main category of items being moved, approximate quantity and any stairs, access or special handling considerations.
